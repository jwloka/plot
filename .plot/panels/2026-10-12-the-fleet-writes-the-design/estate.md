# Estate juror: the-fleet-writes-the-design

Position: amend

The plan's goal fits D10 and DESIGN-phases.md §6, and most names it cites exist on `main` (316d3de0e). But the estate already holds three of the things the plan builds: the spike's dispatch eligibility (`sliceVerdict` since #1634), a spec-section reader (`specOf` in the needs-design entry), and a fleet-started per-plan agent with a one-run store, a start rule and a *Waiting on you* row (`auto-verify`). The plan names two of the six `plan.phase !== 'approved'` guards in `rules/auto-dispatch.ts`, and changing only those two lets a spike start without its brief being checked. The fleet reads plans on the default branch only (D13), so a `Review: pr` plan in Design is invisible to `designWork` while its plan PR is open, and the plan's first spec destination never fires. The `Parallel agents` claim cites a comment and does not describe what the cap counts. The plan has no Acceptance section. Each defect has a small fix, so the position is amend, not reject.

## Findings

Evidence: `packages/domain/src/rules/eligible.ts:127-134` admits a `design` plan's slice where `spike === true`, `spikeApproved === true` and the slice names one branch; commit 364290a49 (*Dispatching the spike*, #1634) merged `feature/the-spike-can-be-dispatched`, and `plot-fleet-scan.sh:4573-4576` feeds the spike readings into the verdict column. Evidence: `packages/domain/src/rules/auto-dispatch.ts:531` (`planAutoDispatch`), `:582` (`startableBranches`), `:617` (`skippedClaimedBranches`), `:656` (`firstBrieflessBranch`), `:733` (`skippedPlans`), `:798` (`dispatchCandidates`). `packages/fleet/src/shared/auto-dispatch.ts:333-335` builds `missingBriefs` from `dispatchCandidates` only.
