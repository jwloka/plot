2. **`needsDesign` names no write target, and D13 makes the target binding.** Plan `:62` says what the transition writes, not on which ref or through which commit. A `Review: pr` Draft plan lives on its `idea/` branch until its plan PR merges. 3. **DESIGN-phases.md contradicts the revised plan.** `DESIGN-phases.md:140` says "`/plot-idea`'s ceremony step asks" the design question. Plan `:64` moves it out of the ceremony questions to keep Principle 10. The plan cites DESIGN-phases.md as its spec (`:40`), so the spec line must change with it.
4. **The upgrade note's restoring declaration hides every story in the sibling's Discovery tab.** Changelog `:18` offers `Discovery = plan:draft; Design = plan:design; Development = plan:approved; Testing = plan:delivered; Released = plan:released` to "keep the earlier columns". 5. **Three view decisions stay in components.** CLAUDE.md *Every rendered state is a domain property* and DESIGN-phases.md §8 ("Every view state in this story is a domain property") rule out a decision made in `.tsx`. The board slice puts new decisions there: `Board.tsx:224`'s checklist "renders in the phase that holds `plan:delivered`" (plan `:56`), the no-badge rule at `Board.tsx:217-219` and `Swimlanes.tsx:161-163`, and `isDraft`/`isApproved`/`isReadyToStart` re-derived in `PlanCard.tsx`. Plan `:52` cites that plan only for its empty-column measurement.
