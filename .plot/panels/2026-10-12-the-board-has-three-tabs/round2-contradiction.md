# Contradiction juror, round 2: the-board-has-three-tabs

Position: amend

The revision applies all eight of my round-1 amendments, and almost every estate claim is true on `main` at `553c4777b`. The revision also brings new contradictions. First, the inbox cap is an open point, but `tickets-and-stories-have-lanes` uses this plan's Delivery slice as the precondition that makes its project-wide Jira read safe. With the cap still in place, a matched Jira reader on a large project can lose their own tickets. Second, the plan now matches an undeclared GitHub login. `DESIGN-phases.md` §5 defines that reader as unmatched, and the plan does not list that spec change. Third, the plan narrows D9's *Only my work* and changes its *Active sprint* toggle into a button with no recorded decision. Fourth, the plan says "the server and the payload do not change", but `ColumnSchema` strips `releasedOnly`, and `waitingTickets` needs an issue source that `/api/fleet` does not carry. Each defect has a contained fix, so the position is amend.

## Findings

1. **High: the Jira inbox can narrow at the lanes upgrade, because the cap open point is unresolved.** Evidence: plan:64 and :87 say `waitingTickets` runs in the client over at most 50 rows of `/api/fleet`. Plan:87 also says "an assigned ticket older than the 50 newest is not in `/api/fleet`". `tickets-and-stories-have-lanes.md:110` caps the inbox at 50, newest first, after the planned filter. `:277` makes `feature/the-lanes-read-the-whole-jira-project` wait on this plan's Delivery slice, as the precondition. `DESIGN-phases.md` §5 *Scope* says "The drop lands only after the Delivery tab's assignee filter exists". After that slice merges, a matched Jira reader's rows are the 50 newest tickets of the whole project, filtered to that reader. Today the reader gets their own 50 newest (`plot-host.sh:4684`). On a project with 1000 open tickets, this can be zero tickets. The hidden count is not silent, but the count names other people's tickets, not the reader's lost ones. That defeats the reason for the wait, and it breaks D9's "only tickets assigned to the current user". Checked by reading both plans and `fleet.ts:192`.
2. **Medium: the plan's unmatched GitHub reader contradicts `DESIGN-phases.md` §5, and the plan does not name this as a cross-file change.** Evidence: §5 *An unmatched reader* says "when the reader's host login resolves to no declared handle ... shows every ticket ... for example *jwloka is not declared in `People`*". Plan:53 says an undeclared login resolves to itself and owns its tickets, and plan:60 makes "no host login" the only unmatched case on GitHub. These two rules give different results for this repository with no `People` entry: per the spec, every ticket and a notice; per the plan, 0 tickets and "127 hidden". Plan:135 lists only two cross-file changes not made (§5 *Delivery* verification sentence, lanes spike line), and this is not one of them. The plan's reasoning (D21 permits it, and it agrees with `prOwnership`, `ownership.ts:66-72`) is sound. The spec must say the same thing.
3. **Medium: the plan changes two D9 statements and records no decision.** Evidence: D9 (`STORY-...md:84`) says "*Only my work* filters tickets, branches, PRs, plans and builds" and "An *Active sprint* toggle". `DESIGN-phases.md` §5 *Delivery* repeats both. 4. **Medium: the plan says the payload does not change, but it must.** Evidence: plan:45 says "the server and the payload do not change". `ColumnSchema` (`packages/board/src/contract/schema.ts:772-780`) is a `z.object` that lists `checklist` and no `releasedOnly`. The ticket arm's `source: 'github-issues' | 'bitbucket' | 'jira'` (plan:51) is not the domain's vocabulary. `IssueSource` (`rules/issue-source.ts:24-27`) answers `tracker`+scheme, `git-host` or `nobody`. `TRACKER_LISTERS` (`tracker-listers.ts:14-17`) has no `bitbucket` scheme.
