# Deliverable juror: the-fleet-writes-the-design

Position: amend

The design follows D10 and DESIGN-phases.md §6, and its one wait is already satisfied: `feature/the-spike-can-be-dispatched` merged as #1634 (`364290a49`). But the plan cannot be built and proven from its slice lines as written. It has no `## Acceptance` section and names one test for three Changelog claims. The second wave holds two branches. The spike slice names two of the six `phase !== 'approved'` filters in `rules/auto-dispatch.ts`, and with only those two widened, a Design plan's spike starts without the brief check. No reader feeds `designWork` the spec text or the `Spike approved:` record, and the plan names a Delivery tab that does not exist on main. Each of these is fixable in the plan text, so the position is amend, not reject.
